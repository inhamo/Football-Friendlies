# Guest-first account strategy

## Product position

Friendlies should earn registration after demonstrating value. A new visitor can choose a football role and enter a realistic workspace without an email address. The account invitation appears as a way to protect work already started, not as a gate in front of the product.

This is deliberately ethical persuasion: the interface keeps a visible guest route, does not manufacture urgency, does not preselect consent, and explains what is stored. The goal is informed commitment rather than pressure.

## How the evidence shaped the flow

1. **Autonomy before commitment.** “Explore as guest” remains the strongest first action and explicitly says no email is needed. This supports autonomy and avoids the reactance produced by a forced registration wall.
2. **One small, meaningful choice.** The visitor chooses a role, not a long onboarding questionnaire. That creates relevance and identity while avoiding choice overload and cognitive load.
3. **A credible preview.** A concrete nearby fixture shows the product’s job before asking for trust. Familiar local football cues and repeated exposure build comprehension and comfort.
4. **Progress that belongs to the visitor.** Guest work persists on the phone. Creating an account is framed as keeping that work. This draws on psychological ownership, endowed progress, self-efficacy and goal-gradient evidence.
5. **Ask at the moment of intent.** The persistent “Save progress” invitation is quiet and available after exploration. It does not interrupt browsing; it becomes useful when the visitor has something worth preserving.
6. **Short, conventional forms.** Account creation asks for name, role, email and password. Labels remain visible, errors are specific, and the password can be revealed. Familiar controls reduce learning cost and increase perceived competence.
7. **Trust through clarity.** The interface states why identity data is requested and avoids inflated security claims. Trust is treated as ability, benevolence and integrity demonstrated through behavior.
8. **A clean ending.** Successful account creation returns directly to the person’s existing workspace. The transition preserves continuity instead of replaying onboarding.

## Measurement plan

Track the funnel without treating conversion as the only success metric:

- welcome shown → guest workspace entered;
- guest’s first meaningful action;
- “Save progress” opened;
- account form started → completed;
- return within seven days;
- deletion, sign-out and form-error rates;
- qualitative trust feedback from coaches and players.

The key experiment should compare *when* the save invitation appears, not whether guest access exists. Guest access is a trust promise, not an optimization variable to remove when conversion is weak.
