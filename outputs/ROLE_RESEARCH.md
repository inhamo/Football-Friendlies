# Friendlies role-system research

This evidence pass informed the Player, Referee, Sponsor, Scout and Coach workspaces. It is a product reference, not a claim that Friendlies or a user is accredited by FIFA, UEFA, CAF or The FA.

## Decisions applied in the app

- Player: one international-style CV, registration identity, career and season record, physical/playing details, and no more than eight focused 30-second evidence clips.
- Training: open sessions use **Attend**; approval is requested only for restricted sessions or grounds.
- Referee: appointment fee and transport are visible before applying. Eligible work is ordered using category/certificates, observer rating, experience and distance; the profile resembles an official association record.
- Sponsor: opportunities declare investment range, rights and required report-back before a proposal starts.
- Scout: a declared talent philosophy, structured player CVs, verified match data, short evidence, a private watchlist and private observations.
- Coach: qualifications, player welfare, development planning and safeguarding remain part of the operating model.

2. [FIFA Connect](https://support.tickets.fifa.com/advancing-football/fifa-connect) — connected registration records for football participants.
3. [UEFA grassroots football](https://www.uefa.com/development/grassroots/overview/) — inclusive, safe and player-centred participation.
4. [UEFA elite youth programmes](https://www.uefa.com/development/youth-players/elite-youth-programmes/) — clear development pathways and monitored talent projects.
5. [UEFA Player Careers database](https://intellcentrecatalogue.uefa.com/player-careers/) — career history, profile, demographics and player-use data.
6. [FIFA Talent Development](https://publications.fifa.com/en/talent-development/) — identification, training, playing opportunity and transition stages.
7. [The FA Introduction to Talent Identification](https://help.thefa.com/support/solutions/articles/7000094708-what-is-the-introduction-to-talent-identification-) — a rounded view of player potential and scout responsibilities.
8. [UEFA Youth B Diploma syllabus](https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/0268-11efc151e48c-550ddc2c7a6a-1000/uefa_youth_b_diploma_2020_en_20210401154446.pdf) — technical, physical, psychological and social player development.
9. [The FA Youth Development Rules](https://www.thefa.com/TheFA/RulesandRegulations/~/media/Files/PDF/the-fa-2012-13/2012-13-rules/youth-development-rules.ashx) — football philosophy and multidimensional recruitment profiles.
10. [UEFA Academy](https://www.uefa.com/development/our-support/academy/) — professional development, administration and education across football roles.
