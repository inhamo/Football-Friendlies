# Grassroots v2 — UI/UX evidence base

69 sources, each coded to one main theme. Gathered 26–27 Nov 2026.


## Designing for people who read slowly or not at all (11)

- [Medhi, Sagar, Toyama – Text-free UIs for illiterate and semi-literate users (MSR)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/?p=160568) — Text-free designs were strongly preferred over text-based ones; a consistent help feature matters
- [Medhi et al. – Designing mobile interfaces for novice and low-literacy users (ACM ToCHI)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/?p=161317) — Study of 90 users in India, Kenya, Philippines, South Africa: text forms unusable for first-timers, error-prone for novices
- [Medhi Thies – UI design for low-literate and novice users: past, present, future](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/?p=167992) — Review of non-text input/output combinations for minimal-training use
- [MSR project – UIs for low-literate users](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/uis-low-literate-users/) — Low literacy links to difficulty with hierarchical navigation, not just reading
- [Knoche & Huang – Text is not the enemy](https://vbn.aau.dk/ws/files/309756266/TextIsNotTheEnemy_NUI_Workshop.pdf) — Illiterate users still use some text (names, numbers) – don't remove all words
- [NN/g – Writing for lower-literacy users](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/writing-for-lower-literacy-users/) — Simplified content: low-literacy users beat high-literacy users on the old site
- [TechWhirl – writing for struggling readers](https://techwhirl.com/writing-technical-content-for-low-literacy-and-struggling-readers/) — Aim for a 6th-grade reading level on main screens
- [health.gov – users with limited literacy skills](https://odphp.health.gov/healthliteracyonline/2016/what-we-know/full/) — Limited-literacy users are mostly on mobile phones
- [UserTesting – low-literacy content tips](https://www.usertesting.com/blog/low-literacy) — Writing for low literacy helps high-literacy readers too
- [Hill & Simha – literacy app for adult learners (GWU)](https://www2.seas.gwu.edu/~simha/research/jen-chi.pdf) — Simple designs were intuitive and learnable even for inexperienced adults
- [Emmanuel & Muyingi – mobile UI for low-literacy users, rural South Africa](https://ajol.info/index.php/gjmas/article/view/91045/80482) — Localised UI that matched cultural experience was accepted by users

## Voice, sound and WhatsApp habits (10)

- [Rest of World via NextNature – WhatsApp voice notes in Senegal](https://nextnature.net/magazine/story/2023/whatsapp-voice-notes-revolutionize-senegal-s-agricultural-landscape) — Voice notes let low-literacy farmers take part fully
- [NANOG list – formal literacy not needed for digital literacy](https://seclists.org/nanog/2021/Mar/418) — People who never went to school use WhatsApp at speed through voice notes
- [Remitly – why some cultures prefer voice notes](https://blog.remitly.com/technology/voice-notes-vs-text-why-some-cultures-prefer-to-speak) — Voice avoids typing in a second or third language
- [LILT – Africa redefining usability (voice-native, multilingual)](https://lilt.com/blog/africa-redefining-ai-usability-multilingual-multimodal) — WhatsApp voice + photo tools work with low literacy and code-switching
- [CSIR – voice service in South Africa](https://researchspace.csir.co.za/bitstream/10204/5897/1/Sharma_2012.pdf) — Voice interfaces can reach users text can't
- [UFH – GUI vs voice for rural micro-entrepreneurs](https://libdspace.ufh.ac.za/bitstreams/a70a1b8e-94c7-4e64-a79d-48d14dff97ff/download) — Keypad (DTMF) input beat voice-only input: tap with sound, don't force speaking
- [MDN – Web Speech API](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Web_Speech_API) — Free text-to-speech built into browsers
- [Readium – speechSynthesis across browsers](https://readium.org/speech/docs/WebSpeech.html) — Most Android voices have offline variants
- [WhatsApp FAQ – click to chat](https://whatsapp.com/faq/en/general/26000030) — wa.me/?text= opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message – free, no API
- [Sinch – wa.me links are free](https://sinch.com/engage/free-whatsapp-link-generator/) — Creating WhatsApp links costs nothing

## Trust, mistakes and money fears (EcoCash) (10)

- [Yale – mistake-friendly design](https://usability.yale.edu/ux/best-practices/mistake-friendly-approach) — Let people undo and change course without fear
- [aufait UX – older adults' digital confidence](https://www.aufaitux.com/blog/ux-design-older-adults-digital-confidence/) — Fear of irreversible mistakes; offer undo, review, cancel
- [ExplorAR (arXiv) – trial and error builds confidence](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.01282) — Safe exploration reduces fear, especially around money
- [St Andrews – accidental touches](https://digitalcommunications.wp.st-andrews.ac.uk/2022/08/09/design-to-avoid-catastrophes-when-accidental-touches-happen) — Bumps on a kombi cause mis-taps; make actions reversible
- [HKUST – 'I do not dare to try and ask'](https://xrim.hkust.edu.hk/static\publications\daretotry\I_do_not_dare_to_try_and_ask_IJHCI_.pdf) — Low self-efficacy stops people trying features
- [Sikayetvar – EcoCash complaints summary](https://www.sikayetvar.com/en/ecocash-us) — Failed transfers, hard reversals, slow support, fraud, outages
- [Bulawayo24 – EcoCash 'login loop'](https://bulawayo24.com/index-id-news-sc-national-byo-261517.html) — Users want clear updates when money is deducted
- [Techzim – refund charges for failed transactions](https://techzim.co.zw/2018/12/ecocash-should-refund-ecocash-charges-to-customers-for-failed-transactions-agreed) — Charged for failed transactions plus the 2% tax
- [Bulawayo24 – wallets drained, sent to police](https://bulawayo24.com/index-id-news-sc-national-byo-260974.html) — Fraud fear: never ask for PINs or OTPs
- [Techzim – EcoCash bot can't help](https://techzim.co.zw/2018/04/whats-going-on-with-ecocash-transactions) — Support bots that can't solve problems make people angrier

## Fonts, size, contrast and colour (10)

- [Braille Institute via Wikipedia – Atkinson Hyperlegible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atkinson_Hyperlegible) — Free (OFL) font built so letters can't be mistaken for each other
- [Adobe – Atkinson Hyperlegible Next](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/atkinson-hyperlegible-font) — 2025 'Next' version: 7 weights, 150+ languages
- [Material Design blog – Atkinson design](https://material.io/blog/atkinson-hyperlegible-design) — Distinct letterforms and wider spacing stop letters blurring
- [TypeDrawers – Lexend's evidence](https://typedrawers.com/discussion/comment/43203) — Lexend's reading claim rests on one study of 20 children – weaker evidence
- [Google – touch target size](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/7101858) — At least 48dp touch targets
- [GOV.NZ – touch targets](https://govtnz.github.io/web-a11y-guidance/ka/accessible-ux-best-practices/mobile-apps/mobile-app-a11y-by-feature-type/touch-and-pointer-target-sizing.html) — Test with thumbs; one-handed use
- [MDN – colour contrast](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Accessibility/Understanding_WCAG/Perceivable/Color_contrast) — 4.5:1 minimum, 7:1 enhanced
- [GOV.UK accessibility blog – colour contrast](https://accessibility.blog.gov.uk/2016/06/17/colour-contrast-why-does-it-matter) — 1 in 12 men has colour vision deficiency; never rely on colour alone
- [Sherwin-Williams – colour across cultures](https://www.sherwin-williams.com/architects-specifiers-designers/education/sw-article-pro-coloracrosscult) — In parts of sub-Saharan Africa red can signify mourning
- [UX Collective – colour meaning per culture](https://uxdesign.cc/how-the-meaning-of-colour-varies-per-culture-14afd8c9734f) — To fans, colours mean clubs – pick a neutral, league-wide palette

## Simple steps, flat menus, one thing per screen (8)

- [MSR – Helping the low-literate navigate UIs (CHI 2013)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/?p=287) — People with little schooling struggle with deep, hierarchical menus
- [Nielsen via OSU – lower-literacy reading behaviour](https://desis.osu.edu/seniorthesis/index.php/2021/01/25/lower-literacy-users-writing-for-a-broad-consumer-audience/) — They read word by word, lose place when scrolling, pick the first search hit
- [Jakob Nielsen – Jakob's Law](https://jakobnielsenphd.substack.com/p/jakobs-law) — People expect your app to work like the apps they already use (here: WhatsApp)
- [Laws of UX – Jakob's Law](https://lawsofux.com/jakobs-law/) — Familiar patterns cut learning effort
- [GOV.UK Service Manual – form structure](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/form-structure) — Start with one thing per page
- [GOV.UK Design System – question pages](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/question-pages) — One question per page helps people focus
- [UK Parliament design system – forms](https://designsystem.parliament.uk/how-tos/designing-forms/) — Low-confidence users find one-thing-per-page easier; avoid ALL CAPS
- [DWP accessibility manual](https://accessibility-manual.dwp.gov.uk/guidance-for-your-job-role/interaction-designer) — One thing per page lowers cognitive load

## Cheap data, cheap phones, offline (7)

- [Google Design – offline design](https://design.google/library/offline-design) — People switch data off to save money; design for offline
- [Google Design – cost transparency](https://medium.com/google-design/nurture-trust-through-cost-transparency-b61a5947d2fc) — Showing data cost builds trust
- [Google Design – connect no matter the speed](https://medium.com/google-design/connect-no-matter-the-speed-3b81cfd3355a) — Show what's happening instead of a blank screen
- [Google – Building for Billions](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/building-for-billions/) — Store data, queue requests, do core tasks offline
- [web.dev – offline UX guidelines](https://web.dev/articles/offline-ux-design-guidelines) — Provide an offline experience by default
- [Meta Engineering – building Facebook Lite](https://engineering.fb.com/android/how-we-built-facebook-lite-for-every-android-phone-and-network/) — Stay tiny, design for 2G
- [Google Design – making YouTube Go](https://design.google/library/making-youtube-go) — Tell people exactly how much data something costs

## Zimbabwe: languages, PSL look, free tools (7)

- [Wikipedia – Languages of Zimbabwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Languages_of_Zimbabwe) — Shona and Ndebele are the main languages; English is the lingua franca
- [UNICEF – Language and learning, Zimbabwe](https://www.unicef.org/esa/sites/unicef.org.esa/files/2018-09/UNICEF-2017-Language-and-Learning-Zimbabwe.pdf) — ~75% speak Shona, ~17% Ndebele
- [TheGlobalEconomy – Zimbabwe literacy](https://www.theglobaleconomy.com/Zimbabwe/Literacy_rate/) — Adult literacy ~90% – so design for low confidence and second-language reading, not just illiteracy
- [Wikipedia – Zimbabwe Premier Soccer League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwe_Premier_Soccer_League) — League runs as the Castle Lager PSL (Delta Beverages)
- [AB InBev brand world – colour palette](https://brandworld.ab-inbev.com/color) — Parent-brand gold/red/blue reference values
- [Firebase pricing](https://firebase.google.com/pricing/?hl=en) — Spark plan free: 50K reads/day; phone-SMS login is billed – so use PIN login
- [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/) — Free hosting with unlimited bandwidth

## Pictures and icons (and when they fail) (6)

- [Medhi et al. – Mobile money-transfer UIs for non-literate users (CHI 2009)](https://microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/medhi_chi2009.pdf) — Hand-drawn pictures beat photos/icons; voice helps; numbers are widely readable
- [Zender & Cassedy – icon comprehension in Tanzania vs USA](https://journals.uc.edu/index.php/vl/article/view/5880) — Most 'universal' icons were not understood across cultures
- [arXiv – predatory apps and limited literacy](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.17025) — A lock icon read as 'access denied'; icons need context
- [Wan, Chang, Sengupta – icon taxonomy for semi-literate users](https://aisel.aisnet.org/amcis2013/HumanComputerInteraction/RoundTablePresentations/1) — Concrete, context-specific pictures perform best
- [Chua et al. – population-stereotyped icons](https://core.ac.uk/download/301365308.pdf) — Icons drawn from local ideas work better
- [Sengupta & Chang – icon styles and low-literate users](https://core.ac.uk/download/301359628.pdf) — Icon style affects whether people keep using the app
