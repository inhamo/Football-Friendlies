## No-shows, late cancellations, drop-outs (13)

- [Footy Addicts blog – organise games](https://blog.footyaddicts.com/?p=1150) — Paying before the match = more reliable players, fewer drop-outs
- [Footy Addicts – no trading spots](https://footyaddicts.uservoice.com/knowledgebase/articles/1977000-no-trading-selling-spots-why) — Late arrivals are marked no-shows, which affects reliability and future access

- [Footy Addicts – move your game management](https://blog.footyaddicts.com/move-your-game-management-with-footy-addicts) — Organisers: chasing payments, drop-outs with no warning, stuck in one WhatsApp group
- [BenchApp Finder](https://www.benchapp.com/find-a-game/soccer/buffalo-ny.md) — Teams come up short every week; finder only shows games inside your travel radius
- [Clapton CFC – forfeit](https://www.claptoncfc.co.uk/?p=32059) — Club spent a night trying to find last-minute friendly opponents – too late
- [LCSL committee thread](https://groups.google.com/g/lcslcommittee/c/8NjEDhSZ1MU) — Last-minute cancellation with no apology dumped admin on a volunteer at work
- [The JC – cup no-shows](https://www.thejc.com/life/sport/cup-competitions-completely-devalued-by-no-shows-b62a0yhk) — Sides pull out at the 11th hour; player availability is the root cause
- [Brampton Adult Soccer – forfeit protocol](https://bramptonsoccer.com/bas-forfeit-protocol/) — 48-hour notice rule; forfeits notify opponents and cancel refs automatically
- [Try Tag Rugby – forfeit policy](https://www.trytagrugby.com/london-super-league-forfeit-policy/) — Penalties scale with how late the notice is
- [Powerleague – Ringerz](https://www.powerleague.com/blog/powerleague-launches-ringerz) — 84% of 5-a-side teams hit a last-minute drop-out; fix runs on WhatsApp
- [The42 – @Latecallup](https://the42.ie/2181196) — Games get cancelled needlessly when a public pool of subs could fill gaps
- [Pivio – organiser guide](https://pivio.pages.dev/blog/football-organiser-guide/) — Visible countdowns and a reliability record reduce flakes
