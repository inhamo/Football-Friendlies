# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Grassroots football coaches, players, referees, league organisers, and club managers across Zimbabwe who coordinate weekend football on phones, often outdoors and between other responsibilities. Coaches and organisers need creation and management tools, players need availability and lineup tools, and referees need assignments, reporting, and result-verification access.

## Product Purpose

Friendlies is the operating layer for community football in Zimbabwe: one place for teams to find opponents, arrange and verify matches, manage squads, join local leagues, and keep captains and players aligned. It replaces fragmented WhatsApp, Facebook, and personal-contact coordination with a trusted grassroots network. Success means fewer hours spent chasing opponents, fewer cancellations, and more players getting a fair game every weekend.

## Brand Personality

Neighbourly, dependable, spirited. Friendlies should feel rooted in Zimbabwean football communities while carrying the clarity and confidence of a professional match-day tool.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic global score apps, betting products, esports neon, corporate league-management software, and social feeds that bury the task in content. Do not imitate the supplied blue football reference literally; retain its sporting energy while building a distinct local, trust-first identity.

## Design Principles

1. Get to a credible opponent in seconds.
2. Show why a match is a good fit, not just that it is available.
3. Use reliability internally to protect the community; show verified behaviour and clear commitments instead of public percentage scores.
4. Keep match logistics concrete, local, and easy to scan.
5. Reward positive football behaviour more prominently than punishment.
6. Keep permissions and workspaces appropriate to the active Coach, Player, or Referee role.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, large touch targets, screen-reader labels, reduced-motion compatibility, and layouts that remain legible in bright outdoor light. Never rely on colour alone for status. Use plain language that works for users with varied digital confidence and allow space for future localisation.
