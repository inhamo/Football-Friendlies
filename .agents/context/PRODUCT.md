# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Grassroots football players, coaches, referees, scouts and sponsors in Zimbabwe, often using small phones, limited data and informal grounds without formal addresses or facilities.

## Product Purpose

Grassroots helps local football communities find one another, organise matches and competitions, communicate safely, record verified results and build a shared playing history.

## Brand Personality

Community-proud, energetic and practical. It should feel close to the football people actually play, not like a formal professional-league system.

## Anti-references

Avoid corporate SaaS styling, professional-club luxury, betting-app noise, placeholder statistics, cluttered forms and assumptions that every team has money, facilities or a formal address.

## Design Principles

- Make the next football action obvious on small screens.
- Show real community information and honest empty states.
- Let guests understand the value before asking them to register.
- Keep team, match and personal information clearly separated.
- Protect youth participants and private conversations by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, readable controls on small phones, reduced-motion support, non-colour status cues and flows that remain usable with slow or interrupted connections.
